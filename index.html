🎺 Instrument Letter Generator

One student per line, 6 fields separated by a tab (copy from a spreadsheet) or a comma:
First name(s) | Last name(s) | Instrument | Homeroom # | Homeroom teacher(s) | School
The school (Hawthorne, Whittier or Lowell) sets the teacher contacts and first-lesson time. Names are capitalized automatically; hyphenated last names are kept. Separate two homeroom teachers with "/" or "and". Click into any field on a letter to edit it before printing. Rows with extra columns (e.g. the old form-response format) are rejected.
